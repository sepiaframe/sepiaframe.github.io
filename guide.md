### 1. Portrait & Branding Shot List
- **The "Classic" Headshot:** Eye-level, looking at the camera, clean background.

- **The "Worker" Shot:** Action shots of you doing what you do (typing, sketching, etc.).

- **The "Casual" Lean:** Relaxed posture against a brick wall or fence.

- **The Detail Shot:** Close-up of your hands, tools, or accessories.

- **The Wide Environmental:** You in your favorite Calgary neighborhood (e.g., Inglewood or East Village).

### 2. Family Session Shot List
- **The "Christmas Card" Shot:** Everyone looking and smiling (we get this one first!).

- **The Walk-and-Talk:** The family walking hand-in-hand toward or away from the camera.

- **The Candid Chaos:** Kids playing, parents laughing—the "real" moments.

- **Individual Portraits:** Just the kids, just the parents, and each child alone.

- **The "Lift:** Parents lifting smaller children for those high-energy smiles.

### 3. Event & Wedding Highlights

| Moment          | Why it Matters                                                                 |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| The Atmosphere  | Wide shots of the venue and decor before guests arrive.                          |
| The Key Players | Candid shots of the guest of honor or the happy couple.                          |
| The "Vibe"      | Real laughter, toasts, and reactions during speeches.                            |
| Small Details   | Close-ups of flowers, rings, or branded event materials.                         |


### 4. Calgary-Specific Prep Tips
Note to Clients: Calgary light is beautiful but harsh. I always recommend "Golden Hour" (one hour before sunset) for that soft, Alberta glow.

- **Layer Up:** Even in July, a Calgary evening can get chilly. Bring a stylish jacket or sweater that matches your outfit.

- **The Wind Factor:** If we are shooting near the Bow River or up at a viewpoint like McHugh Bluff, expect a breeze! Hair ties or hairspray are your best friends.

- **Footwear:** If we’re heading to Fish Creek or Glenmore Park, bring comfortable walking shoes and swap into your “photo shoes” once we reach the spot.
