#### Calgary Urban (The "Vibe" of the City)
- **East Village & St. Patrick’s Island:** Perfect for a modern, architectural feel. The skipping stone bridge and the Simmons Building provide a great mix of industrial and natural riverfront.

- **The Peace Bridge (Sunnyside):** An iconic Calgary staple. Best shot at dusk when the red glow of the bridge contrasts with the blue hour sky.

- **Stephen Avenue:** Ideal for high-fashion or corporate branding shoots. The historic sandstone buildings and the "Galleria Trees" offer a sophisticated urban backdrop.

- **Central Library:** A futuristic masterpiece of wood and glass. Note: Indoor professional shoots require advanced booking with the Calgary Public Library.

#### Calgary Parks (Natural Beauty, Close to Home)
- **Scotsman’s Hill (Ramsay):** The classic "Calgary Skyline" view. Perfect for sunset portraits with the Saddledome and downtown in the background.

- **Crescent Heights Hill:** Offers a sweeping panoramic view of the Bow River and the city.

- **Fish Creek Provincial Park:** Massive and diverse. Whether you want deep forest, open yellow grasslands, or creek-side rocks, this is the best "nature" spot within city limits.

#### Day Trip: The Rockies (West)
- **Banff Townsite & Vermilion Lakes (1.5 hrs):** Mount Rundle reflecting in the lakes is the quintessential Alberta shot.

- **Moraine Lake & Lake Louise (2 hrs):** The famous turquoise water.

  -2026 Pro Tip: Personal vehicles are restricted; we must book the Parks Canada shuttle or a private tour well in advance.

- **Kananaskis – Wedge Pond (1 hr 15 mins):** A quieter alternative to Banff. The reflections here are stunning, and it’s much easier to find a private spot for a session.

#### Day Trip: The Badlands (East)
- **Drumheller – Horseshoe Canyon (1.5 hrs):** It feels like stepping onto another planet. The layered red rock and deep canyons provide incredible shadows and textures for dramatic portraits.

- **The Hoodoos:** Small, iconic sandstone pillars. Best for quick, high-impact "otherworldly" shots.

#### Day Trip: The Deep South (South)
- **Waterton Lakes – Prince of Wales Bluff (3 hrs):** Known for its high winds and epic drama. The view of the historic hotel perched above the deep blue lake is unmatched in the province.

- **Red Rock Canyon:** Vibrant red stone walls and emerald water. It’s a bit of a drive, but the colors are a photographer's dream.

#### Permits & Logistics (The Fine Print)
- As your photographer, I handle the heavy lifting, but please keep the following in mind for 2026:

- **City of Calgary Parks:** Some locations (like Devonian Gardens) require a permit fee (approx. $58.49/hour).

- **National Parks:** Sessions in Banff or Waterton require a Parks Canada Discovery Pass for your vehicle.

- **Travel Fees:** Locations within Calgary are included in my standard rates. For Day Trips (Kananaskis, Banff, Drumheller, etc.), a modest travel fee applies to cover fuel and time.
